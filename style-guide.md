# Addis Tours — design notes

## Brand

The site presents **Addis Tours with The Addis Guide**, using the supplied Addis Guide logo and the public profile [@the_addisguide](https://www.instagram.com/the_addisguide/) as its source for positioning and service categories.

The profile describes the promise as “Explore Addis. Experience Ethiopia.” and highlights city tours, Ethiopian food, culture, private tours and local guiding. Recent public posts frame Addis as a meeting of modern life and Ethiopian culture, with street stories and music as part of the city experience. The page uses that framing without adding prices, schedules or unverified tour details.

## Visual direction

- Cream, forest green, warm red and muted gold echo the supplied logo and create an editorial travel-journal feel.
- Arial and system sans-serif fonts carry through the full page, following the original clone's typography.
- Local photography shows Addis at dawn, a city street and an Ethiopian coffee moment.
- Two attributed clips from the public @the_addisguide reels provide Addis footage in the hero and full-screen story scenes. Scrolling gently shifts and scales the media throughout the page; section copy reveals as it enters view. Videos load near the viewport, pause offscreen and remain off when reduced motion is enabled.
- The experience ribbon and orbital details loop continuously, cards ease/zoom on hover, city notes reveal in sequence, and FAQ answers animate open.
- Layouts collapse from editorial split sections and multi-column cards to a single-column mobile experience.

## Page and interactions

1. Hero — “Explore Addis. Experience Ethiopia.” with direct tour-planning links.
2. City introduction — local context and an Addis street image.
3. Experiences — filterable city, food, culture and private day-trip cards with detail dialogs.
4. City notes — coffee, culture and an evening in Addis.
5. Guide section — introduces the local-guiding offer.
6. Plan builder — selectable interests and a copyable message prompt; contact is completed through Instagram.
7. FAQ and Instagram links — answers based on public profile information and links to the original posts.

The site does not collect or submit form data, accept bookings, or process payments. Availability and tour details should be confirmed directly with the guide through Instagram.
